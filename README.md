# Smart CV Matcher

Building AI course project

## Summary

Smart CV Matcher is an AI tool that helps job seekers find suitable jobs by comparing their skills, qualifications, and experience with job descriptions. It aims to make job searching easier and help recruiters identify suitable candidates.

## Background

Finding the right job can be difficult and time-consuming. Job seekers often apply for positions that do not match their experience, while recruiters may receive hundreds of applications.

This project explores how AI could improve the recruitment process by matching candidates with suitable job opportunities. It is an interesting way to use AI to solve a practical problem in the workplace.

## How is it used?

Users provide their CVs and preferred job roles, locations, and other requirements. The AI analyses their experience and skills and compares them with available job descriptions.

The system then recommends suitable vacancies and explains why each job might be a good match. It could also suggest skills that candidates may need to develop.

The main users would be job seekers, recruitment agencies, and HR departments.

## Data sources and AI methods

The system could use CVs provided by users, publicly available job advertisements, job descriptions, and information about professional skills.

Natural language processing could extract relevant information from CVs and job advertisements. Text similarity techniques could compare candidate profiles with job requirements and rank potential matches.

Personal data would need to be handled securely and with the users' permission.

## Challenges

The results would depend on the quality of the CVs and job descriptions. Some candidates might have relevant skills that are not clearly mentioned in their CVs.

The system could also produce biased recommendations if the data used to develop it is biased. Privacy and data protection would be important.

AI should support recruitment decisions rather than replace human judgement.

## What next?

The project could be developed into a simple website where users upload their CVs and receive job recommendations.

Future features could include CV improvement suggestions, interview preparation, skills-gap analysis, and training recommendations.

The system could eventually support multiple languages and job markets in different countries.
